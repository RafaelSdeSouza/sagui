# Bagpipes

Sagui returns integrated regional photometry that can be fitted with
Bagpipes. This example shows region 8 of Sagui-8.

![Bagpipes fit to the photometry of Sagui-8, region
8.](../reference/figures/sagui8_region8_bagpipes_sedfit.png)

Bagpipes fit to the photometry of Sagui-8, region 8.

Sagui provides the regional fluxes and uncertainties; Bagpipes fits the
physical model.
