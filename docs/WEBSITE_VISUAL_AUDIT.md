## Functional checks

- Exact primary navigation order: Get started, User guide, Examples, Reference,
  Paper, GitHub.
- Mobile navigation opens and exposes all top-level links; User guide remains a
  labelled submenu.
- Native light and dark controls both render without overflow.
- Typed search for `segment_regions` returns exact and sparse references plus
  related guides.
- Keyboard focus on search has a visible 3 px coral outline.
- Browser console recorded no errors or warnings.
- Internal-link checker passed 56 HTML files, including fragments and redirect
  targets.
- Legacy anchors `#sagui-10-main-example`, `#sagui-11-faint-bridge`, and
  `#target-sn-selection` exist on the new Examples page.
- `/examples.html` and `/python.html` preserve query strings and incoming
  fragments.

## Scientific visuals

- The real manuscript mosaic renders at its natural aspect ratio without
  cropping and remains the primary homepage evidence.
- The first run uses a fixed-seed 24 × 24 × 9 simulated galaxy with bulge, disc,
  and knot SED profiles. The high-resolution map-plus-SED panel is generated by
  `tools/make_website_example.R`; its table is stored at
  `docs/website-audit/sagui-first-run-regional-seds.csv`.
- The Sagui-10 target-S/N panel and Sagui-8 Bagpipes SED plot are linked to their
  producer scripts and machine-readable tables where available.
- Unresolved source-cube and per-panel configuration gaps remain explicit in
  `docs/website_image_provenance.csv`.
